People

A lot of my life in mathematics has been about finding people to think with.

MathModern

I came up with MathModern and then organized it with friends. It’s a Telegram forum dedicated to professional mathematics, with more than 1,000 members as of September 2026.

Higher Geometry

I founded and administer Higher Geometry, a Russian-speaking mathematical community with a Telegram channel and a forum accessible through its bot.

Since the end of my bachelor’s studies, I’ve organized seminars with other students and PhD students. Recordings are on YouTube.
